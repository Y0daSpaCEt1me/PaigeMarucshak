# Quantum model calibration

The public story contains a fictional company, not reported company statements or a forecast. Two sheets share six inputs and a three-year income statement, cash-flow statement and balance sheet. Equity funding restores a $50M reserve; financing is an assumption, not a guarantee.

Peer checks: [IonQ 2025 results](https://investors.ionq.com/news/news-details/2026/IonQ-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2025-Financial-Results/) report approximately $130M revenue and $306M R&D. Its consolidated operations and acquisition/noncash effects limit comparability. [Quantinuum prospectus](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2110105/000162828026041003/quantinuum-424b4.htm) reports 2025 revenue of about $31M and R&D of $165M. These anchor a plausible order of magnitude, not expected returns or growth.

The invented opening assumptions use $50M revenue, $180M R&D, $25M sales and $45M administration. Growth, margins, spending, capex, compensation and cash remain scenario inputs. Stock compensation substitutes for cash compensation within the expense budget; it does not change total operating expenses, but changes cash flow and dilution. Assumptions exclude acquisitions, debt, dividends and tax-loss carryforwards. No peer accounts appear on the public slides.

Validation: 27 scenarios reconcile assets to equity and opening cash plus free cash flow plus financing to closing cash. Browser checks at desktop and mobile verify two sheets, six controls per sheet, multiple live output changes and native wheel navigation.
